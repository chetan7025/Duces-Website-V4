FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Setup in the UAE: FAQs.

What is the difference between mainland and free-zone setup?

Mainland and free-zone companies operate under different regulatory and licensing frameworks. The suitable route depends on business activities, operating plans and applicable authority requirements.

Can a foreign investor establish a UAE company?

Foreign ownership is available for many UAE business activities, subject to the applicable legal framework, activity restrictions and authority requirements. The position should be checked for the specific proposed activity.

What documents are generally required for company formation?

Requirements vary by jurisdiction, legal structure and activity. Identification documents, application details and supporting business information may be required. Additional documents may apply depending on the case.

Does company formation automatically include a residence visa?

No. Visa eligibility, allocation and processing are subject to the selected setup package and applicable immigration and authority requirements.

Can DUCES assist with business banking after setup?

DUCES can provide business banking consultancy and help clients understand documentation and application considerations. Account opening decisions remain with the relevant bank.
