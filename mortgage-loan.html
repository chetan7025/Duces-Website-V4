Mortgage Documentation

Documents Commonly Required

Documentation depends on the applicant's employment status, residency, property and finance provider. The following are indicative examples.

  • Passport and valid identification
  • Emirates ID and UAE visa, where applicable
  • Salary certificate or employment confirmation, where applicable
  • Recent personal bank statements
  • Salary slips, where requested
  • Existing loan and credit commitment details
  • Business financial records for self-employed applicants
  • Property details and purchase documentation
  • Down payment and source-of-funds evidence, where requested

This is a general checklist. A finance provider may request additional documents or verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mortgage Finance FAQs

Common questions about property finance in the UAE.

Can expatriates apply for a mortgage in the UAE?

Eligible expatriates may be able to apply for property finance, subject to the lender's criteria, property eligibility and applicable regulations.

Can non-residents obtain a UAE mortgage?

Some providers may offer products to eligible non-resident applicants. Availability, property eligibility, down payment and documentation requirements vary.

Can self-employed business owners apply?

Self-employed applicants may be considered by providers that accept their profile. Business financial records, bank statements and operating history may be requested.

How much down payment is required?

The required contribution depends on the applicant's profile, property value, property type, residency status and applicable finance rules. Confirm the current requirement with the lender.

How long does mortgage approval take?

Processing time varies according to the provider, applicant documentation, property valuation and other checks. No fixed approval timeline can be guaranteed.

Can I apply if I already have existing loans?

Existing financial commitments may be considered during affordability assessment. The lender will review your complete financial position.

Does DUCES guarantee mortgage approval?

No. DUCES provides consultancy, preparation and coordination support. Mortgage approval, rates, terms and final decisions are determined by the relevant finance provider.

Can DUCES help me understand mortgage documents?

We can help you organise commonly requested information and understand the general application process. The lender should clarify its final contractual and product terms.
