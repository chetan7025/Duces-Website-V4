FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Consultancy FAQs.

What services does DUCES Consultancy Services provide?

DUCES provides business setup consultancy, business banking consultancy, business and mortgage finance coordination, accounting, bookkeeping, VAT, corporate tax and PRO-related support.

Can DUCES assist with UAE company formation?

Yes. We provide consultancy and coordination support for Mainland, Free Zone and Offshore company formation requirements.

Does DUCES provide business bank account assistance?

We assist with understanding banking requirements, documentation preparation and coordination with relevant financial institutions. Final account opening decisions rest with the bank.

Can DUCES help with VAT and corporate tax?

We provide consultancy and coordination support relating to VAT, corporate tax and accounting requirements through applicable service channels.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee approval?

No. Company licences, bank accounts, financing, visas and tax-related decisions remain subject to the relevant authority or provider's requirements and final approval.
