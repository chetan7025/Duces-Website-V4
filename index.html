COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can DUCES guarantee a business bank account?

No. Financial institutions conduct their own due diligence and make independent decisions. DUCES can help explain requirements and coordinate documentation, but cannot guarantee approval.

Can you help compare Free Zone and Mainland?

We can discuss activity, ownership, visa and premises requirements. Final eligibility and licensing terms are confirmed by the relevant authority.

Do you support existing UAE companies?

Yes. Contact us about banking, finance enquiries, accounting, tax, documentation and corporate services.

What should I share for an initial consultation?

Start with your business activity, whether the company is new or existing, the service you need and your preferred contact details.
