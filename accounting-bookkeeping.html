FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Accounting & Bookkeeping: FAQs.

What is the difference between accounting and bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping focuses on recording and organising financial transactions. Accounting involves interpreting, reviewing and presenting financial information. The scope of each service depends on the agreed engagement.

Why does a UAE business need organised financial records?

Organised records help business owners monitor financial activity, understand expenses, review transactions and prepare relevant information for business and tax purposes.

Can a small business use bookkeeping support?

Yes. Businesses of different sizes may benefit from maintaining structured transaction and financial records. The appropriate scope depends on the business's needs.

Can you help organise existing accounting records?

Existing records can be reviewed to understand their current structure and identify the support required. The scope will depend on the availability and condition of the information.

Does bookkeeping support include VAT filing?

Bookkeeping and VAT filing are different activities. VAT-related support should be discussed separately to establish the required scope and applicable responsibilities.
