FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PRO & Visa Services: FAQs.

What are PRO services in the UAE?

PRO services generally involve coordination and support relating to business documentation and applicable government procedures, such as licence, establishment and immigration processes.

Can a newly established company apply for employee visas?

Visa eligibility and allocation depend on the company's licence, establishment status, applicable rules and relevant authority requirements. Approval is not automatic.

What documents are required for a UAE employment visa?

Requirements depend on the applicant, employer, visa category and applicable procedure. Identification and employment-related information may be required, along with additional documents where applicable.

Can you assist with visa renewals?

DUCES can discuss the applicable documentation and process coordination requirements for eligible renewal cases.

Does PRO support guarantee visa approval?

No. PRO coordination and documentation support do not guarantee approval. Final decisions are made by the relevant government authorities.

Can individuals enquire about family visa procedures?

Yes. You can discuss your requirements to understand the relevant eligibility considerations and documentation process for your circumstances.
