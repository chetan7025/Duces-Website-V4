Preliminary Assessment

What Finance Providers May Consider

Each provider has its own eligibility framework. These are common areas that may be reviewed.

Business Performance

Company operating history, turnover, profitability, business activity and financial performance.

Banking Conduct

Account history, transaction activity, average balances, liabilities and repayment behaviour.

Ownership & Documentation

Shareholding structure, authorised signatories, business records and supporting documentation.

Meeting general criteria does not guarantee eligibility, an offer or approval.

Financial Documentation

Prepare Your Business Finance Documents

A complete and consistent document set can help finance providers assess a business more effectively. Exact requirements depend on the provider and facility.

  • Valid UAE Trade Licence
  • Company constitutional documents, where applicable
  • Shareholder and authorised signatory identification
  • Recent business bank statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Financial statements and management accounts, where available
  • Sales invoices and business contracts, where requested
  • Details of existing finance and liabilities
  • Clear explanation of funding purpose

Additional information, security or guarantees may be required by the finance provider.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business Loan FAQs

Common questions about business finance in the UAE.

Can a newly established company obtain a business loan?

Some finance providers may require an established operating history, while other products may have different criteria. Eligibility depends on the provider and the company's circumstances.

How much business finance can a company obtain?

The available amount depends on the provider's assessment of turnover, cash flow, profitability, liabilities, security and other relevant factors. No amount can be guaranteed in advance.

Are bank statements required?

Business bank statements are commonly requested to understand account activity, cash flow and financial conduct. The required period varies.

Are VAT returns important for business finance?

VAT returns may help demonstrate reported business activity and turnover where applicable. Providers may request additional financial evidence.

Does DUCES guarantee business loan approval?

No. DUCES provides consultancy, preparation and coordination support. All lending decisions, terms and approvals are determined by the relevant finance provider.

Can an existing business with finance explore additional funding?

It may be possible, depending on the company's financial position, existing obligations and the provider's eligibility criteria.

Can I use business finance for working capital?

Certain facilities may support working capital requirements. The permitted use depends on the product and the finance provider's terms.
