IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

Every Business Has Different Tax Requirements.

Tax obligations should not be determined only by the type of licence or the size of a business. The applicable rules and individual circumstances need to be considered.

  • Business legal structure and activities
  • Nature and level of taxable transactions
  • VAT registration position and applicable thresholds
  • Corporate tax treatment and relevant exemptions
  • Financial records and supporting documentation
  • Applicable filing periods and deadlines

Tax treatment should be assessed using current UAE legislation and the facts of each business.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

VAT & Corporate Tax: FAQs.

Are VAT and corporate tax the same?

No. VAT is a tax on relevant supplies of goods and services, while corporate tax applies to taxable business income under the UAE corporate tax framework. Their rules and obligations are different.

Does every UAE business need to register for VAT?

Not necessarily. VAT registration depends on the applicable registration rules, taxable supplies, imports and relevant thresholds. Some businesses may also qualify for voluntary registration.

Does every UAE company have corporate tax obligations?

UAE corporate tax rules may apply to taxable persons according to their legal status and circumstances. Registration and filing requirements should be checked for the specific entity.

Are free-zone companies automatically exempt from corporate tax?

No. A free-zone company must meet applicable conditions to benefit from the qualifying free-zone tax regime. Its status and income treatment need to be assessed under the relevant rules.

What records should a business maintain?

Relevant records may include sales and purchase invoices, bank statements, expense documents, accounting records and other supporting information required under applicable rules.

Can bookkeeping and tax support be discussed together?

Yes. Accounting and tax-related requirements are connected. You can discuss both areas together to understand the scope of support your business may need.
